    Alhambra: how to actually get in

    You are booking three days out for the hardest ticket in Spain

    The Nasrid Palaces admit 300 people per half hour slot, and the standing advice for July and August is to book six to eight weeks ahead. General tickets (~€19–22, covering Nasrid Palaces, Alcazaba and Generalife) are very likely gone for your window. Three routes are still realistic.

    1. Nasrid Palaces by night — Tuesday to Saturday, 22:00–23:30 in summer, around €9. Less contested than daytime, and in a 38 °C week it is the better visit anyway.
    2. Generalife and Alcazaba only — around €10–13, no Nasrid Palaces, sells out later than the general ticket. Combine with a night visit and you have reassembled the full ticket.
    3. A guided tour holding pre-booked entry — GetYourGuide, Civitatis, Viator. €30–60 per person, a real markup, but they have space when the official site shows none.

    Official site only: tickets.alhambra-patronato.es. Cancellations get released, so check more than once today. Passports are scanned at every entry point and names must match, so buy in all four names.
